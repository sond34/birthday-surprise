Do you love me? ❤️

My buji (pari),

Our story didn’t start with love at first sight—it started with time… and time gently chose you for my heart.

Do you remember 2nd January 2024?
Tarun bhaiya’s birthday.
The first time I saw you.

That day, I didn’t fall for you.
It was just a quiet curiosity—
“Oh… who is he?”

Time passed slowly, softly.
Somewhere between teasing, laughing, talking, and just being around you, something inside me started changing. I didn’t understand it at first. I was confused—was it attraction, or was it something more?

What truly pulled me towards you was your heart.
The way you loved your brother.
The care and respect you showed your sister-in-law.
So attentive. So gentle. So real.
I remember thinking, “Awww… how can someone be this caring?”

Still, I stopped myself.
I told my heart no.
You were my best friend’s brother.
And from the way you spoke, I believed you had no interest in me anyway.

So we stayed there—
teasing each other, pretending nothing was happening—
while my heart quietly kept choosing you.

Then came 13th April 2025.
Abhishek’s birthday.

That night changed everything.

We were drunk.
We were close.
We ended up on the sofa—
me resting on your chest,
You're holding me.

And that was the moment I fell for you.

You held me so close to your heart.
You kept kissing my forehead again and again,
as if you were scared to lose me.
As if letting go wasn’t even an option.

You could have crossed the line.
You could have blamed the alcohol.
But you didn’t.

You chose control.
You chose respect.
You chose to be a gentleman.

That choice made me feel safe.
That purity made me fall deeper.

From there, everything grew naturally.
Talking. Knowing each other.
Sharing our past, our present, our thoughts, our fears.

Friend.
Best friend.
Lover.
And now… everything.

I love how real we are.
No sugar-coating.
No fake promises.
Just honesty, purity, and effort.

From stealing time for each other
to openly make time for us—
Our bond didn’t rush.
It grew.

After you, everything I ever craved—
love, care, desire, respect, security—
I found it all in one person.
And even more than I dreamed of.

I want to cherish you.
Our bond.
Every moment that made us us.

I want to love you like this forever.
I want this love to grow every single day.
I want to fall in love with you again and again—
every day, every moment—
the same way I fell for you the very first time.

I promise you care when you’re tired.
Security when you overthink.
Reassurance when your heart needs it.
Respect for every version of us.

What we share is divine—pure and steady like a heartbeat, meant to live as long as my heart does.

Always yours.
Only yours.
Your Suyu 🤍

Happy Birthday My Love ❤️

You are my forever, my peace, my heart 🎂🌹
